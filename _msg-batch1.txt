feat(rooms): structured replies, per-member models, retry, context budget

First batch of docs/collaboration-rooms-optimization.md.

L1.1 structured replies (cc_remote/wrapper/room_plan.py)
- A member may close its answer with a fenced cc-plan block; the room lifts
  summary/steps/risks/needs into card data (RoomMessage.plan) and shows the
  prose. The parser never raises: a malformed block leaves the text untouched
  and is not retried, because a formatting mistake must not cost a turn.
- Room.plan_requests (default on) adds a two-line instruction to the prompt.

L4.2 context budget
- Entries the user quoted explicitly are skipped: they are already in the ask, so
  paying for them twice is waste.
- The automatic transcript is now filled newest-first under CONTEXT_BUDGET
  (6000 chars, CC_REMOTE_ROOM_CONTEXT_BUDGET) instead of by entry count only.
- When explicit quotes are present the automatic depth drops to 2.

L4.1 per-member model/effort
- RoomMember gains model/effort, forwarded through RoomMemberSpec and
  _spec_to_member into _spawn (which already accepted both), so advice can run on
  a strong model while execution runs on a cheap one.

L3.3 failure retry (cc_remote/wrapper/room_retry.py)
- Permanent conditions (missing directory, unbound DSH member, invalid session)
  are never retried; connection-ish ones are retried exactly once after a pause.
- The dispatcher's failure handling moved into finally: previously a failure
  raised while handling a failure left the member at "running" forever with no
  timeline entry. That is the bug behind "空 AssertionError" style reports, and
  the finally block makes the record and the permit release unconditional.

Retry delay is injectable so tests do not race a real 2s sleep.
Tests: 94 pass, 3 skipped (12 plan, 6 retry, 11 host, 18 dispatch incl. budget,
dedupe and failure-visibility assertions).
